# FusionFall image sources

These images document KC Wolff-Ingham's work on Cartoon Network's original
FusionFall project. Copyright remains with Cartoon Network or the credited
creators.

- `planet-fuse.webp`: Matthew Schwartz, original FusionFall team portfolio,
  <https://www.behance.net/gallery/24007843/Game-FusionFall-2006-2009>
- `character-creation.webp`: Matthew Schwartz, original FusionFall team
  portfolio,
  <https://www.behance.net/gallery/24007843/Game-FusionFall-2006-2009>
- `social-play.webp`: WIRED launch coverage, image credited to Cartoon Network,
  <https://www.wired.com/2008/11/get-a-sneak-pee/>
- `infected-zone.webp`: WIRED launch coverage, images courtesy Cartoon Network,
  <https://www.wired.com/2008/09/hands-on-fusion/>

Three further images are published in the case study but are not external
sources — they are artifacts of the project's own research program, and no
capture credit is recorded for them here:

- `playtest-session.webp`: a recorded play session, with the participant's
  camera view beside the gameplay.
- `research-model.webp`: the research model mapping player experience across
  FusionFall locations.
- `pens-findings.webp`: the PENS findings chart (autonomy rising while
  competence fell in the more challenging infected zones).

The playtest capture shows an identifiable participant, so its usage and
consent basis belongs with the file before it is reused anywhere beyond this
case study. Add an entry here if an original credit is identified later.
